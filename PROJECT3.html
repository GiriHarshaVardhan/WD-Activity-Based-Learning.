Admissions Procedure

Admissions are strictly merit-based. Prospective students must follow these steps:

  • Submit the online application form along with previous educational transcripts.
  • Provide valid entrance examination scores (e.g., JEE Main).
  • Attend the institutional counseling session for seat allocation.
  • Complete fee payment and document verification.
